## Introduction

Logs is a record of what changed in your account today. Each time a silo is created, updated, or deleted, an entry is added, no matter whether the change came from the console, the Manage API, or an AI tool over MCP.

Use it to answer questions like "who changed this silo?" and "did my script run?".

## Open Logs

Click **Logs** in the sidebar, under **Observe**.

Each entry shows:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Activity** | What happened, in plain words. |
| **Action** | The kind of change, such as create, update, or delete. |
| **Silo** | The silo the change applies to. |
| **When** | How long ago it happened. |

Entries are grouped by time, with the most recent first.

## Filter by Action

Use the filter at the top of the screen to show one kind of activity: **All**, **Created**, **Updated**, **Deleted**, or **Other**.

## Where a Change Came From

The action tells you which surface made the change.

| Action | Source |
|---|---|
| **Create**, **Update**, **Delete** | The console |
| **Create (API)**, **Update (API)**, **Delete (API)** | The [Manage API](/api-reference/introduction) |
| **Create (MCP)**, **Update (MCP)**, **Patch (MCP)**, **Delete (MCP)** | An AI tool over [MCP](/mcp/overview) |
| **Schema Update**, **Schema Remove** | A change to a silo's [schema or lock](/features/schema-lock) |

## Limitations

- Logs show **today's** activity only. They reset each day.
- The screen shows the most recent 50 activities.
- Reads are not logged. To see how often a silo is read, use [Analytics](/features/analytics).
