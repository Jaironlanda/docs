## Introduction

This page is a guide on how to delete a silo through the user console.

## Delete silo

<Warning>Once Silo is deleted, there is no going back. Please be certain.</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the silo's settings">
    Click **Silos** in the sidebar, open the silo you want to delete, and click the **Settings** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Delete silo">
    At the bottom of the tab, click the `Delete silo…` button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the deletion">
    A confirmation dialog shows the silo's name, region, and size. Type the silo name to confirm, then click `Delete silo`.

    <Tip>Click the silo name in the dialog to copy it.</Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

The silo and its data are removed, and its URL stops working. Any app that still reads the URL receives a `404` response.

## Other ways to delete a silo

- **Manage API**: Send a [Delete Silo](/api-reference/endpoint/delete-silo) request with a Manage Key.
- **MCP**: Ask a connected AI tool with a **Read & write** key to delete it. See [MCP Tools](/mcp/tools).
