## Introduction

This page provides a comprehensive guide on how to update silo data through the user console.

## Update the data

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the editor">
    Click **Silos** in the sidebar, open the silo you want to update, and click the **Data & schema** tab. You can also click `Edit content` in the silo's header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the JSON">
    Edit the JSON in the **Data** pane on the left. You have two ways to do it:

    - **Edit**: Type directly in the editor. The footer shows **Valid JSON** when the document parses, or the number of syntax errors when it doesn't.
    - **Upload**: Switch to the upload mode and drop a `.json` file, or click `Browse files`. The file replaces the editor's content. Nothing is saved until you click `Save data`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your changes">
    Switch to the **Changes** mode to see a diff of what you changed against the saved version. Click `Discard` to throw the changes away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click `Save data`, or press `⌘S` on macOS or `Ctrl S` on Windows and Linux. The new data is served from your silo's URL.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>If the silo has a locked schema and your data doesn't match it, the console won't save. It tells you which value is invalid so you can fix it. See [Schema Lock](/features/schema-lock).</Note>

For everything the editor can do, see the [Data & Schema Editor](/editor/json-editor).

## Update the name, access, or region

Open the silo's **Settings** tab. See [Manage Silo](/how-to/manage-silo#3-settings).

## Other ways to update a silo

- **Manage API**: Send an [Update Silo](/api-reference/endpoint/patch-silo) request with a Manage Key.
- **MCP**: Ask a connected AI tool to update or patch the silo. See [MCP Tools](/mcp/tools).
