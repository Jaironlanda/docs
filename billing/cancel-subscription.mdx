## Introduction

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to cancel your subscription plan in JSONsilo.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Billing Page">
    Click **Billing** in the sidebar to view your billing details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel Subscription">
    Scroll to the bottom of the page and click `Cancel subscription`.

    {/* TODO: screenshot of the Cancel subscription link at the bottom of the Billing page. Suggested path: /images/billing/cancel-subscription.png */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    A dialog will appear to confirm your cancellation. Click `Cancel subscription` to confirm, or `Keep subscription` to go back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens After You Cancel

You'll keep access until the end of the current billing period, then your plan will move to Free.

Until then, the **Billing** page shows the date your access ends.

## Resume a Cancelled Subscription

Changed your mind? As long as the billing period hasn't ended, you can undo the cancellation.

Open **Billing** and click `Resume subscription` under **Current plan**. Your subscription continues as before.
