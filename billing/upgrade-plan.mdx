## Introduction

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to change your subscription plan in JSONsilo.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Billing Page">
    Click **Billing** in the sidebar to view your billing details. You can also click the plan badge next to the JSONsilo logo.

    The top of the page shows your **Current plan**, when it renews, and your **Usage**: how many silos you have used out of your plan's limit. On the free trial it also shows your trial requests left and when the trial ends.

    {/* TODO: screenshot of the Billing page with the Current plan and Plans sections. Suggested path: /images/billing/billing-page.png */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Plan">
    Under **Plans**, compare what each plan includes. Your current plan is marked **Current plan**, and the next plan up is marked **Next step up**.

    Click the `Choose plan` button on the plan you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Change">
    What happens next depends on whether you already have a subscription.

    - **No subscription yet** (free trial or legacy Free plan): You are taken to the checkout to enter your payment details.
    - **Existing subscription**: A dialog asks you to confirm the switch. Click `Confirm switch`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How You Are Charged When You Switch

- **Upgrade**: You'll be charged a prorated amount on your next invoice, or immediately if the billing interval changes.
- **Downgrade**: You'll receive a prorated credit toward your next invoice.

## Before You Downgrade

A smaller plan has lower limits. Check [Pricing Plans](/pricing-plans) before you switch, in particular the number of silos and the size per silo.

Features that your new plan doesn't include stop being available:

- **Schema Lock**: An existing lock keeps protecting the silo, but you can no longer change the schema. See [Schema Lock](/features/schema-lock#plans-and-downgrades).
- **MCP**: Your MCP Keys are kept but stop working until you upgrade again. See [MCP Keys](/keys/mcp-keys#if-your-plan-changes).

## Manage Your Payment Method and Invoices

On the **Billing** page:

- Click `Manage payment method` to update the payment method used for your subscription.
- Click `Manage subscription` to open the customer portal.
- Scroll to **Billing history** to see your past invoices and orders. Click `Receipt` to open one.
