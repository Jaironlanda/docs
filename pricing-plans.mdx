## Free trial

Every new account starts on a free trial with full access, so you can try the paid features before you choose a plan.

The trial ends after **2 days** or **1,000 requests**, whichever comes first.

- Requests to your silo URLs, the Manage API, and MCP tool calls count towards the 1,000 requests. Using the console does not.
- The sidebar shows **Trial requests left** and **Trial time left** while the trial is active.
- When the trial ends, requests to your silos return `402` until you choose a plan. See [Rate Limits](/rate-limits#when-the-trial-ends).
- Your silos carry over when you pick a plan.

To choose a plan, open **Billing** in the console. See [Change Plan](/billing/upgrade-plan).
